# OG Image Design Brief: Omni Store Online

## General Specifications
- **Dimensions**: 1200 x 630 pixels (Aspect Ratio 1.91:1)
- **Format**: PNG or High-quality JPG
- **File Size**: Under 300KB for fast loading

## 1. Homepage OG Image
- **Background**: Modern, clean gradient (e.g., Deep Navy to Vibrant Blue) or a lifestyle shot of diverse products.
- **Logo**: Large, centered or top-left.
- **Tagline**: "India's Ultimate Online Shopping Destination."
- **CTA**: A "Shop Now" button graphic (not clickable, but visual).
- **Branding**: "Free Delivery | Best Prices | Secure Payments" icons at the bottom.

## 2. Product Page OG Image (Dynamic Template)
- **Layout**: Split screen or Product-Focused.
- **Left/Center Side**: High-resolution product image on a white or light grey background.
- **Right/Bottom Side**:
  - **Product Name**: Bold, clear font.
  - **Price**: "Now only ₹XXXX" (Dynamic).
  - **Store Branding**: Omni Store Online logo in the corner.
- **Badge**: "In Stock" or "Exclusive Deal" sticker graphic.

## Best Practices
- **Safe Zone**: Keep all text and important elements within the central 1000x500px area to avoid cropping on various social platforms (LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook).
- **Contrast**: Ensure text is highly legible against the background.
- **Consistency**: Use the same brand colors and typography as the website for a seamless brand experience.
