# SEO Keyword Strategy: Omni Store Online

## Primary Keywords (High Buyer Intent)
- **"online store India"**: Broad term for general visibility.
- **"shop [product category] online India"**: Targeted for specific aisles (e.g., "shop electronics online India").
- **"buy [product] online cheap India"**: Price-sensitive shoppers looking for deals.
- **"best online shopping India"**: High-volume generic search term.
- **"Omni Store Online"**: Brand-specific keyword to dominate the knowledge panel.

## Long-Tail Keywords
- **"buy [product] with free delivery India"**: Focus on the free shipping benefit.
- **"affordable [category] online India"**: Targeting budget-conscious demographics.
- **"best [product] price India 2025"**: Temporal searches for current models/trends.
- **"[product] COD available India"**: Crucial for the Indian market where Cash on Delivery is preferred.

## On-Page Placement Guide
- **H1 Tag**: Every page must have exactly one H1. 
  - *Home*: "Omni Store Online | India's Most Trusted Online Shopping Destination"
  - *Product*: "[Product Name] — Buy Online at Best Price"
- **H2/H3 Tags**: Use for subcategories or product features.
- **Product Titles**: Follow the formula: `[Brand] + [Product Name] + [Key Specification/Color]`.
- **URL Slugs**: Maintain clean, descriptive URLs. 
  - *Good*: `/shop/electronics/sony-wh-1000xm5-headphones`
  - *Bad*: `/product/id-9823472`
- **Image Alt Text**: Use descriptive text for screen readers and SEO.
  - *Format*: `[Product Name] - Omni Store Online`

## E-commerce Specific SEO Tips
1. **Pagination**: Use `rel="canonical"` to the first page or "view all" page to prevent duplicate content issues.
2. **Aggregate Ratings**: Implement customer reviews to display gold stars in Google search results, boosting CTR.
3. **Internal Linking**: Use breadcrumbs to create a strong hierarchical link structure.
4. **FAQ Schema**: Add frequently asked questions (e.g., "What is the return policy for [Product]?") to product pages to expand search real estate.
5. **Merchant Center**: Sync your product feed with Google Merchant Center to appear in the "Shopping" tab.
